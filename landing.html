Maturity Model

Read your talent engine at a glance

Each band is a different operating reality. The value is not the label; it is knowing where to focus next: foundations, integration, or optimization.
Nascent
0–10 points

Talent attraction is mostly reactive. Your brand story lives in slide decks and conversations, not in your actual talent ecosystem.

  • Limited or no documented EVP.
  • Careers site and JDs are generic or outdated.
  • Little to no consistent employer brand content.
Developing
11–19 points

You have strong ingredients, but they are fragmented. There is clear opportunity to connect brand, channels, and data into one coherent system.

  • EVP exists but is unevenly used.
  • Some campaigns perform well, but insights are not scaled.
  • Candidate experience varies by recruiter or business unit.
Advanced
20–26 points

Employer brand, recruitment marketing, and candidate experience are working together with intentionality. Focus shifts to precision and experimentation.

  • Insight-driven EVP is embedded across the funnel.
  • Channel and content performance, backed by analytics, guide investments.
  • Candidate feedback and data drive continuous improvement.
